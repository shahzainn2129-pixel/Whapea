Privacy policy

Vynk respects your privacy.

What we collect

Username, name, email, profile photo and bio. Your posts, comments, likes and follows. Your online status and last seen, only if you keep them on.

Chats

Nearby chats over Bluetooth stay between the two phones. Internet chats are delivered through our service and are removed from it once delivered. Your chats are kept on your own phone.

Posts and stories

Posts are removed after 7 days. Stories end after 24 hours (48 with Plus).

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads based on it, or read your contacts or location without your permission.

Permissions

Microphone for calls and voice notes. Camera for photos and video calls. Bluetooth and nearby devices for nearby chat. Notifications for alerts. Each is used only for that purpose.

Your control

Private account, block, report, hide last seen and online status, and delete your account (your profile and posts are removed).

Children

Vynk is not for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will tell you and this page will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/shahzainn2129-pixel/Whapea.
